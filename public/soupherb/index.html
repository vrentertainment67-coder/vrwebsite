The label

Ash Roy and Calm Chor started Soupherb in 2013, when India's experimental techno and minimal producers had nowhere to put their records out. Thirteen years on it's an international home for forward-thinking artists, touring Asia and Europe.

The name came from a friend who always spelt "superb" as "soupherb".
